C. PRIMARY ESTIMATOR EVALUATION（两个量严格区分）

   actual_context = count(SEP.join(render_j for packed))  —— 对拼接串整体实测，非加性预测

   sep_inc = count(render_i + SEP + render_{i+1}) - count(render_i) - count(render_{i+1})
